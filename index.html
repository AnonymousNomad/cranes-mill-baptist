About us

A family around the cross.

We are a Bible-believing church committed to worshiping God, loving people, and serving our community with the hope of Jesus Christ.

Belong. Grow. Serve.

Whether you are visiting for the first time, returning to church, or looking for a place to grow, you are welcome here. Our gatherings are warm, our teaching is grounded in Scripture, and our ministries are built around real relationships.
