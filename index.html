// who we are

A Church Rooted in
Scripture & Community

Cranes Mill Baptist Church is a Bible-believing congregation serving God on the southwestern shores of Canyon Lake. We are 15 minutes from New Braunfels and 25 minutes from San Antonio — close enough to the city, quiet enough to hear God.

For decades, we've been a place where families grow, faith deepens, and community matters. We are not a perfect church — we are a faithful one. We gather to worship, to learn, and to serve one another and our neighbors.

Whether you've been in church your whole life or have never set foot in one, you are welcome here.

"Therefore go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit." — Matthew 28:19
Scripture-centered — Every sermon, every study, rooted in the Bible.
Family-focused — Programs for every age, from children to seniors.
Community-driven — We serve Canyon Lake through outreach and partnership.
